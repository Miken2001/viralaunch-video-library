# Kinetic Quote

**Use it for:** famous quotes, scripture verses, philosophy, a founder's statement, a single
bold claim — calm, editorial, shareable.

**Do not use it for:** lists, data or anything needing more than ~40 words on screen.

## How it looks
Large serif words fade up one by one (key words in italic accent color), a giant faint
quotation mark sits behind, then the attribution signs in under an accent rule.
Palettes: `ivory`, `night`, `oxblood`, `sage`. No captions (the quote *is* the text).

## Filling the props (fill tier)
- `quote`: ≤ 40 words. Wrap 1–3 key words in `*asterisks*` to emphasise them.
- Attribution must be real. If a famous line is a paraphrase or disputed, say so in `context`.
- Narration (optional): read the quote, then the author.

## Product placement
`<AdWeave>`: end-card or sponsor-mention. For a brand statement, use `native-integrated`
with the brand's own words.

## Forking ideas
Add a slow background photo with `SceneAsset`, typewriter reveal, or split long quotes into
two cards.

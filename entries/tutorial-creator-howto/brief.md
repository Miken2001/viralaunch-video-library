# Creator How-To

**Use it for:** friendly step-by-step tutorials about film, music, games, books and hobby
culture: caring for vinyl, starting a film or book club, setting up a game night, making a
playlist, building a fan-art routine, getting into a new genre. It also fits any light "how
to" where each step is a short instruction plus a small checklist. Works as a whole short or
as the "here is how" beat of a longer video.

**Do not use it for:** rankings or numbered facts (use `listicle-countdown`), money maths
(use `tutorial-finance-money-steps`), sports plays (use `tutorial-sports-play-steps`), or
medical and legal advice.

## How it looks
A warm studio frame: soft drifting colour blobs, a top bar with the kicker and step dots.
Scene 1 shows the kicker, a big serif title whose *accent* words turn italic and get a
hand-drawn underline, and the steps fanning in as floating pills. Each step then whips in:
the headline rises word by word (accent words underlined again), a chat-style tip bubble
slides in, and a floating task card tilts up with an icon, the step label and a progress bar.
Its checklist lines tick and strike through one by one, then a cursor glides in and taps the
action button, which fills with the accent colour and a check. If a scene has an image it
appears as a photo strip inside the card. In landscape the headline and tip sit left and the
card sits right.

## Filling the props
- `title`: what the viewer will learn, max 48 characters, e.g. `How to start a *film club.*`
  Wrap 1–2 words in `*asterisks*` (that word gets the italic accent and underline).
- `kicker`: a 1–3 word topic label, e.g. "Record care", "Game night".
- `steps`: 2–6 steps, in order. Each has:
  - `label`: short step name (max 28), shown on the card and in the intro list.
  - `headline`: the instruction (max 44) with 1–2 `*accent*` words.
  - `items`: 0–4 short checklist lines (max 34 each) that tick off.
  - `tip`: one plain sentence (max 90) for the tip bubble, or "".
  - `action`: a 1–2 word button label the cursor taps ("Done", "Saved", "Booked"), or "".
  - `icon`: one of the kit line icons, e.g. music, camera, book, star, heart, people,
    calendar, clock, check, spark, sun, chat.
- `palette`: `cream` (warm paper, orange), `blush` (pink and sky, raspberry) or `studio`
  (dark, amber).
- Narration: one scene for the title, then one scene per step (scene 2 = step 1). With
  exactly one scene per step there is no title scene. Give each step scene at least 4
  seconds so the checklist and the tap read.

Keep the advice real and widely accepted; do not invent statistics or quote people.

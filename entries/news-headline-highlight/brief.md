# News Headline Highlighter

**Use it for:** news recaps, product announcements framed as news, study results,
historical "front page" moments.

**Do not use it for:** fake news. Real events need a real `source`; recreated historical
pages must say so; never imitate a real newspaper's name, logo or design — use a generic
masthead.

## How it looks
A cream newspaper card slides up with a slight rotation (masthead, dateline, big serif
headline, optional body), the camera slowly pushes in while a highlighter marker sweeps
across each phrase in `highlights` in turn; then the card softens and a dark "WHY IT
MATTERS" card rises.

## Filling the props (fill tier)
- `highlights` must be **exact substrings** of the headline or body (case-sensitive), in order.
- `body` ≤ 2 short sentences. `whyItMatters` is the takeaway.

## Product placement
`<AdWeave>`: sponsor-mention fits a news format. `native-integrated` works for a launch
announcement written as a press item.

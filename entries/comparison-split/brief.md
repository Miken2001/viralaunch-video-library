# Versus Split Screen

**Use it for:** rivalries and head-to-heads — athletes, teams, countries, eras, cities,
products, historical empires. Anything with 2–6 comparable numbers.

**Do not use it for:** more than two subjects (use data-story-bars) or claims you can't source.

## How it looks
Two colored halves slide in from the edges with names/subtitles (and optional scene images
1 and 2 as faded backgrounds), a VS badge pops, then stat rows animate as mirrored bars with
a ● marking the winner of each row; a verdict line lands near the end.

## Filling the props (fill tier)
- Pick contrasting `color`s (hex). Keep names short (≤ 12 chars looks best).
- `stats`: real, comparable numbers; set `lowerIsBetter` for times, prices, errors.
- Narration: intro, the contrast, the verdict.

## Product placement
`<AdWeave>` for topic videos. A `native-integrated` item can compare the user's product to
"the old way" — only with approved, truthful claims; never name a competitor unfairly.

## Forking ideas
Add per-row icons, animate a running score, or turn it into a tournament bracket.

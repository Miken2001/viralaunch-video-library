# Culture Mix Showreel

**Use it for:** showing range across culture: a streaming service's or channel's lineup, a
festival or museum programme, a "classics you should know" mix, a weekly picks newsletter,
a creator's best-of, or a quick tour of films, albums, games, books, art and events. Works
as the hook, an evidence beat or the closer of a longer culture video, and as a native
product video where the brand presents the mix.

**Do not use it for:** one single work told in depth (use a documentary or story template),
data-heavy comparisons (use a data story), or invented releases, dates or makers. Every
title, year and name must be real and checkable.

## How it looks
A deep gradient stage with glossy floating spheres that drift in parallax, a thin orbit
ring with a small orb circling the stage, and dust. The intro shows `brandName · N picks`
and the `title` popping word by word over a fan of small cards that spring up from below.
The fan then flies into a 3D carousel: one tall 9:16 card in the centre, its neighbours
tilted and dimmed at the sides. Each card has art (the scene image with a slow push, or a
drawn motif for its kind: film perforations, equaliser bars, falling pixels, page lines,
paint blobs, sound rings, confetti), a self-drawing line icon, `01/06` microtype, the brand
mark in the corner, one big `word` revealed in a type face that suits the kind, and an info
panel with the title, a pulsing status chip and the meta line. A light sweep and a light
leak mark every swap. Kind chips at the top light up for the current card. At the end every
card gathers into a wall under the `closer` headline and an accent pill with a check mark
pops in with the `tagline`. Pop captions sit under the carousel.

## Filling the props
- `brandName`: who presents the mix (channel, app, festival, show), ≤ 20 characters.
- `title`: the opening hook, ≤ 7 words; wrap one word in `*asterisks*` for the accent.
- `items`: 2–8 works, in the order the narration mentions them. Each needs:
  - `title`: the real name of the work or event.
  - `kind`: one of film, series, music, game, book, art, podcast, event. Mix kinds.
  - `word`: ONE short word stamped on the card (≤ 12 letters), e.g. "Wave", "Ode", "Drop".
  - `meta`: year and maker, or date and venue: "1984 · Alexey Pajitnov".
  - `status`: optional chip text ("Now showing", "New season"); empty shows the kind.
- Narration scenes: one scene per item. Add one extra scene at the start for the intro and
  one more at the end for the closing wall (so 6 items → 8 scenes). With fewer scenes than
  items, the reel spreads the items evenly across the time and still opens and closes.
- Scene images (optional) fill the matching card; without one the card draws its own art.
- `closer`: headline over the closing wall, ≤ 7 words, one `*accent*` word.
- `tagline`: text in the closing pill (product line or call to action).
- `palette`: `lagoon` (teal glass), `noir` (black, cyan orbs), `coral`, `violet`.

## Product placement
Built for `native-integrated`: the brand presents the mix (brand mark on every card, the
closing pill). end-card, mid-roll-card and sponsor-mention also work through `<AdWeave>`;
then use a neutral `brandName` such as the show or list name.

## Forking ideas
Add a calendar strip with weekdays over the wall, a "planned" placeholder card at the end,
or real poster images per card from scene assets.
